# PM2.5 and weather

72 complete days, 2026-09-11 to 2026-11-21 (daily means).

## Spearman correlation with PM2.5

| Variable | Correlation |
|---|---|
| Temperature, °C | -0.48 |
| Wind speed, km/h | -0.66 |
| Relative humidity, % | +0.06 |

## PM2.5 by wind speed

| Wind | Mean PM2.5 | Median PM2.5 | Days |
|---|---|---|---|
| calm (< 5 km/h) | 42.4 | 37.6 | 11 |
| light (5-10 km/h) | 27.0 | 22.7 | 46 |
| windy (> 10 km/h) | 13.4 | 11.4 | 15 |

Correlation is not causation. Over autumn the temperature falls while many other things change (heating, day length), so a relationship with temperature may partly reflect the season.
